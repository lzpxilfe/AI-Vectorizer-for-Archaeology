Rights evidence

USGS rights FAQ:
https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-usgs-topographic-maps-copyrighted?items_per_=&items_per_page=6&page=1

Captured relevant USGS text:
"All topographic maps produced by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) are in
the public domain and are not copyrighted..."

The FAQ states that USGS-produced topographic maps are public domain and not
copyrighted, subject to three exceptions that apply only to US Topo maps from
2009 onward.  This selected product is an 1890 Historical Topographic Map
Collection scan, not a US Topo product, so none of those three exceptions
applies.

The official HTMC collection page also labels its historical-map material
"Sources/Usage: Public Domain." and describes HTMC as scans of maps printed
between 1884 and 2006:
https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/historical-topographic-maps-preserving-past
